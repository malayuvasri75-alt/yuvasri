# Phase 1 – Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Title
PocketSmart AI – Smart Budget & Recommendation Assistant

## Problem Statement
Students and young users often record expenses informally and may not know where their money is going. A simple digital tool can help them record expenses and understand spending patterns.

## Proposed Idea
Build a web application that stores expenses and provides budget-based recommendations.

## Target Users
- Students
- Beginners learning personal finance
- Users who want a simple expense tracker

## Key Ideas
- Expense entry
- Budget comparison
- Category analysis
- Recommendation
- MongoDB storage

## Expected Outcome
A working web dashboard that demonstrates practical use of Python, Flask and MongoDB.
